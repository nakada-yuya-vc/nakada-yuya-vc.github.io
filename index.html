About

I work on payment security and digital identity infrastructure in Japan and the broader APAC region. My focus is the intersection of EMV 3-D Secure, W3C Verifiable Credentials, and how cryptographic trust models can improve data quality and privacy in payment authentication.

I am an individual subscriber of EMVCo and follow the development of payment security standards across EMVCo, the W3C Secure Payment Confirmation CG, and the W3C Verifiable Credentials WG. I am particularly interested in the gap between EU-centric digital identity frameworks such as eIDAS 2.0 / EUDI Wallet and the implementation realities in APAC markets including Japan's Trusted Web initiative.

I write here to think out loud on technical and strategic questions that interest me. These are personal views and do not represent any employer or standards body.
